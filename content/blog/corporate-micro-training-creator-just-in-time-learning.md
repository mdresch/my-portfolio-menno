Most employees have sat through it: a two-hour e-learning course, clicked through as quickly as possible, quiz answers guessed, certificate downloaded, content forgotten by the end of the week. Organizations spend heavily on training like this, yet see little change in how people actually work.

People learn best when they can learn what they need, when they need it, in small doses they can apply right away. This article outlines the **Corporate Micro-Training Creator**: a library of short, targeted training modules — typically five to ten minutes each — designed for just-in-time learning and measurable impact.

## Why Traditional Corporate Training Disappoints

- **Rigid formats:** long sessions that pull people away from their work for hours or days.
- **High costs:** in-person training adds travel, venues, and lost working time.
- **Low engagement:** long modules see poor completion and even poorer retention.
- **Limited scalability:** difficult to roll out consistently across distributed and remote teams.
- **No measurement:** little insight into whether training changed behavior or results.

## The Microlearning Approach

### Short, Focused Modules

Each module covers one clear learning objective in five to ten minutes: how to give constructive feedback, how to spot a phishing email, how to run an effective stand-up, how to use a specific feature in a business system. Learners can complete a module between meetings — or right before they need the skill.

### A Library of 50 Modules

The initial library covers high-demand skill areas such as:

| Skill Area | Example Modules |
| --- | --- |
| **Leadership and management** | Delegating effectively, one-to-one conversations, giving feedback |
| **Communication** | Writing clear emails, presenting with confidence, running meetings |
| **Digital skills** | Collaboration tools, data basics, working safely with AI |
| **Security and compliance** | Phishing awareness, password hygiene, data protection basics |
| **Productivity** | Prioritization, time management, avoiding burnout |

### Built for Retention

Short videos, interactive scenarios, quick knowledge checks, and spaced-repetition reminders help knowledge stick. Learning paths combine modules into structured programs for roles or goals.

### Customizable for Each Client

Organizations can tailor modules with their own examples, terminology, branding, and policies — and commission new modules for company-specific needs.

### Integrated and Measurable

The platform integrates with existing learning management and HR systems, and an analytics dashboard tracks completion, engagement, assessment results, and — where possible — links to business outcomes such as time to competency.

### Flexible Purchasing

Organizations buy only the modules or learning paths they need, which keeps training budgets focused.

## Build Options

| Option | Description | Indicative Investment | Trade-offs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Status quo** | Long-form, generic e-learning. | None | Low engagement, poor results, and client churn |
| **2. Off-the-shelf microlearning platform** | Deliver content on an existing commercial platform. | ~$150K upfront + ~$50K/year | Faster (6-9 months), but limited customization and differentiation |
| **3. Custom micro-training platform with integrated analytics** *(recommended)* | Own platform with customization tools, analytics, and LMS/HR integrations. | ~$300K upfront + ~$40K/year | 12-18 months to build, but differentiated and cheaper to run long term |

## Managing the Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| **Content becomes outdated** | Regular content reviews and modular updates |
| **Low client adoption** | Pilot programs with early clients and clear impact reporting |
| **Integration challenges** | Standard learning interoperability formats and APIs |
| **Bite-sized content too shallow** | Learning paths that combine modules into deeper programs |
| **Development delays** | Phased delivery: content library first, platform features iteratively |

## Measuring Success

| Metric | Baseline | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Training completion rate | 40% | 80% |
| Employee engagement with training | 5 / 10 | 8 / 10 |
| Time to competency for new skills | 8 weeks | 4 weeks |
| Client satisfaction (NPS) | 30 | 60 |
| Pilot clients adopting the platform | — | 70% |
| Annual revenue | $0 | $500K |

## Conclusion

The most effective training is the training people actually complete and use. Microlearning meets employees where they are — in the flow of work, with short, practical lessons they can apply immediately — and gives organizations the data to see what's working.

A curated, customizable library of micro-training modules makes that approach accessible to organizations of every size, turning learning from an annual obligation into an everyday habit.
