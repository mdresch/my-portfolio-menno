Virtual and augmented reality are some of the most effective training tools ever created. A surgeon can rehearse a procedure, a technician can practice a dangerous repair, and a new hire can walk through a factory floor — all without real-world risk. Learners remember more, make mistakes safely, and build confidence before the real thing.

So why isn't immersive training everywhere? Because building it is hard. Custom AR/VR training projects often cost tens of thousands of euros or more, take months to develop, and are built as one-offs that can't easily be reused. This article outlines the **AR/VR Training Scene Builder**: a library of reusable, customizable training scene templates designed to make immersive training faster, cheaper, and scalable.

## Why Immersive Training Is Still the Exception

- **High development costs:** custom simulations require 3D modeling, interaction design, and software development for every project.
- **Scarce expertise:** most training providers don't have AR/VR developers in-house and depend on specialist agencies, with long lead times.
- **No reuse:** a VR safety module built for one factory often has to be rebuilt for the next.
- **A fragmented market:** few providers offer reusable, customizable building blocks.
- **Limited access:** sectors such as education and healthcare often can't justify bespoke immersive content.

## The Solution: Templates, Not One-Off Projects

### A Library of 50+ Training Scenes

Reusable scene templates for high-demand industries and scenarios:

| Industry | Example Scenes |
| --- | --- |
| **Healthcare** | Patient assessment, sterile procedures, emergency response |
| **Manufacturing** | Machine operation, lockout/tagout, quality inspection |
| **Energy and utilities** | Hazard recognition, equipment maintenance, working at height |
| **Logistics and retail** | Warehouse safety, forklift operation, customer interaction |
| **Corporate** | Onboarding tours, soft-skills role-play, emergency evacuation |

### Customization Without Coding

Training providers adapt templates through a configuration tool: swap in their own equipment models, branding, procedures, languages, assessment questions, and scoring rules — without writing code.

### Built-In Learning Features

Step-by-step guidance, branching scenarios, assessments, and analytics on learner performance, exportable to learning management systems.

### Multi-Platform Delivery

Templates run on leading VR headsets and AR devices, with desktop and mobile fallbacks so learners without headsets aren't excluded.

### Flexible Commercial Models

A **subscription** (roughly $500-$2,000 per month depending on scale), **one-time template purchases**, and **premium customization services** for organizations that need bespoke scenarios built on top of the library.

## Build Strategy

| Option | Description | Indicative Investment | Trade-offs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Don't enter the market** | Focus on existing activities. | None | No risk — and no share of a growing market |
| **2. Limited pilot** | 10-15 templates built on existing AR/VR tools to test demand. | ~$500K upfront + ~$300K/year | Lower risk and fast learning, but limited scalability and dependence on third-party tools |
| **3. Full-scale development and commercialization** *(recommended)* | 50+ templates, customization tools, multi-platform integration, and partnerships with platform providers. | ~$2M upfront + ~$800K/year | Higher investment and technical complexity, but the strongest position and revenue potential |

A sensible path combines the two: start with a focused pilot in one or two industries to validate demand and pricing, then scale to the full library once the model is proven.

## Partnerships

Partnerships with AR/VR platform and game engine providers support distribution, co-marketing, and technical integration — and partnerships with training providers and industry associations ensure the templates reflect real training needs and standards.

## Managing the Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| **Slow adoption by training providers** | Pilot programs, free trials, and case studies showing learning outcomes |
| **Rapidly changing hardware** | Build on cross-platform engines and open standards |
| **Technical complexity** | Experienced XR developers and a modular template architecture |
| **Content accuracy in regulated fields** | Subject-matter experts and validation by industry partners |
| **Competition** | Differentiate on customization depth, industry focus, and analytics |

## Conclusion

Immersive training shouldn't be reserved for organizations with large budgets and specialist teams. By turning common training scenarios into reusable, customizable templates, the AR/VR Training Scene Builder lowers the cost and time of immersive learning dramatically.

The result is training that is safer, more engaging, and more effective — available not just to a few pioneers, but to every training provider that wants it.
