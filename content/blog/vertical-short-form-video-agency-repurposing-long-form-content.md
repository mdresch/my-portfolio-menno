Short-form vertical video has become one of the main ways people discover brands. Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts reward content that grabs attention in the first seconds, works without sound, and fits a phone screen.

Meanwhile, most businesses already sit on a goldmine of material they've stopped using: recorded webinars, podcast episodes, conference talks, product demos, and customer interviews. The ideas are there — they're just packaged in a format few people will sit through.

This article outlines a **Vertical Short-Form Video Agency**: a low-overhead service that turns existing long-form content into platform-optimized short videos for businesses that lack the in-house time or skills to do it themselves.

## The Problem

- **Underused content:** long recordings are produced once, published once, and forgotten.
- **No in-house expertise:** editing for vertical formats — hooks, captions, pacing, framing — is a different craft from traditional video.
- **Time pressure:** producing a single short video manually can take a large share of a working day.
- **Fast-moving platforms:** formats, lengths, and algorithms change constantly, and small teams struggle to keep up.

## The Service

### 1. Content Analysis

Review the client's existing library to find the moments with the most potential: strong opinions, clear explanations, surprising facts, emotional stories, and quotable lines.

### 2. Scripting and Hooks

Shape each clip around a strong opening, a single clear idea, and a call to action. Where a clip needs it, write short voice-overs or on-screen text to fill the context that was lost when it was cut from the longer piece.

### 3. Editing for Vertical

Reframe to 9:16, add captions (most short video is watched on mute), tighten pacing, and apply consistent brand styling — fonts, colors, intro and outro elements.

### 4. Platform-Specific Optimization

Each platform has its own conventions for length, captions, hashtags, and posting rhythm. The same clip is adapted for Reels, TikTok, and Shorts rather than cross-posted unchanged.

### 5. Measure and Iterate

Track views, watch time, shares, comments, and follower growth per video, and feed what works back into the next batch.

## Choosing the Model

The business case compares three options:

| Option | What It Means |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Do nothing** | Businesses keep their content locked in long formats; no new revenue for the agency |
| **2. Minimal viable service with basic tools** | Low startup cost and fast to launch, but limited capacity and slower growth |
| **3. Full-scale agency with AI-assisted workflows (recommended)** | AI tools for transcription, clip detection, captioning, and first-pass edits, with human editors handling storytelling and quality |

The recommended option uses AI where it saves the most time — finding candidate clips and generating captions — while keeping humans in charge of judgment: which moments are worth sharing, how they're framed, and whether they fit the brand.

## Roadmap

| Phase | Months | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Foundation** | 1-3 | Define service packages, set up the tool stack, build templates and brand kits |
| **2. Pilot** | 4-6 | Work with a handful of pilot clients, collect feedback, refine workflows |
| **3. Growth** | 7-12 | Scale client acquisition, publish case studies, and expand into new formats and platforms |

## Managing the Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| **Platform algorithm changes** | Spread content across several platforms and keep testing formats |
| **Low engagement on early videos** | A/B test hooks, lengths, and thumbnails; share learnings with clients |
| **Copyright and consent** | Use only content the client owns; get releases for people who appear on camera; use licensed music |
| **Over-reliance on AI tools** | Human review on every video; avoid lock-in to a single tool |
| **Inconsistent quality as volume grows** | Templates, checklists, and a final quality review before delivery |
| **Client churn** | Monthly reporting that ties videos to the client's own goals |

## Conclusion

The best short-form content strategy often starts with content a business already has. An agency that combines AI-assisted editing with human storytelling can turn forgotten webinars and podcasts into a steady stream of vertical video — giving clients more reach from the same ideas, and giving the agency a lean, repeatable service to grow.
